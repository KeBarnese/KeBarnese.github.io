Lascari, Sicily · 5 July 1908

Guardianship-council authorization for Carmela Covelli's marriage

Family-council (consiglio di tutela) deliberation — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

Office of the Conciliator of the Comune of Lascari. In the year nineteen hundred eight, on the fifth of July. Before us, Vincenzo Amato, pharmacist, Royal Vice-Conciliator, assisted by the undersigned Clerk, and under legal authorization of the District Magistrate, we convened the family council in the interest of the minor Carmela Covelli, composed of the following persons:

1. Rosalia Siracusa, daughter of the late Giovacchino — grandmother and guardian;
2. Giuseppe Di Tano, son of the late Emilio — deputy guardian;
3. Salvatore Taormina, son of the late Domenico;
4. Giuseppe Gugliuzza, son of the late Rosario;
5. Giuseppe Cirrito, son of the late Antonino;
6. Antonino Cirrito, son of Giuseppe.

All presented themselves today except one relative, who is found to have emigrated to America. The council was asked whether it consented to authorize the guardian to give consent for the minor Carmela Covelli to marry Salvatore Barnisi, son of Giuseppe. Considering that he is a hardworking young man who can properly provide for his wife and enjoys a reputation of good conduct, the council unanimously resolved to authorize the guardian to consent to the marriage.

Read, confirmed and signed by the office and by the sole council member Antonino Cirrito, all the others being illiterate. A true copy was issued to the applicant, Salvatore Barnisi, at Lascari, July 1908.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record (two consecutive register images). Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily records (Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese).
