Campofelice di Roccella, Sicily · 1887

Birth of Salvatore Muffoletto

Civil birth act No. 27 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

No. 27 — Muffoletto Salvatore, son of Giuseppe. In the year eighteen hundred eighty-seven, on the seventh day of February, at 10 a.m., in the town hall. Before me, Antonino Vaccaro, acting Registrar of the Comune of Campofelice, appeared Giuseppe Muffoletto, aged about thirty-five, shoemaker, resident in Campofelice, who declared to me that at 7 a.m. on the sixth day of the current month, in the house on Via [Costa Garita], to Concetta Di Paola, his wife, a housewife living with him, was born a male child, whom he presents to me and to whom he gives the name Salvatore.

Present as witnesses: two men resident in this comune (one a Di Paola — likely of the mother's family). Read and signed by the declarant, Giuseppe Muffoletto, and by the Registrar, A. Vaccaro.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. The act records the birth as 6 February 1887 (registered the following day) — matching Salvatore's U.S. records exactly. Source: Campofelice di Roccella, Palermo, Sicily civil registration (Tribunale di Termini Imerese).
