Pachino, Siracusa, Sicily · 1888

Birth of Salvatore Barnisi

Civil birth act No. 232 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record. This child is Salvatore (Sam) Barnese, the family's immigrant patriarch.

English translation

No. 232 — Barnisi Salvatore. In the year eighteen hundred eighty-eight, on the thirtieth day of April, at 11 a.m., in the town hall. Before me, Sebastiano Campisi, Secretary delegated (by act of 29 March 1888) as Registrar of the Comune of Pachino, appeared Giuseppe Barnisi, aged fifty, tradesman (industriante), resident in Pachino, who declared to me that at 2 p.m. on the twenty-eighth day of the current month, in the house [number not given], to Carmela Barnirzi, his wife living with him, was born a male child, whom he presents to me and to whom he gives the name Salvatore.

Present as witnesses: Concetto Dugo, aged twenty-two, clerk; and Salvatore Noto, aged twenty-seven, clerk — both resident in this comune. The act was read to those present, who signed it with me except the declarant, who is illiterate. Signed: Concetto Dugo, Salvatore Noto, Sebastiano Campisi. Seen and verified 8 June 1888 at the Secretary's office of Pachino.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. The act records the birth as 28 April 1888 in Pachino (province of Siracusa), registered two days later. It confirms Salvatore's parents outright — father Giuseppe Barnisi, aged ~50 (so born about 1838) and by trade an industriante; mother Carmela, whose maiden surname the clerk here writes Barnirzi — the 1908 marriage act has Barnizri, the same sound with the r and z transposed, since neither Giuseppe (who was illiterate) nor Carmela spelled it themselves. It settles the long-standing question of Salvatore's birthplace: he was born in Pachino, in far-southeastern Sicily, not in the Palermo-province towns of the Covelli and Muffoletto lines. Source: Pachino, Siracusa, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Siracusa, Stato civile italiano, Nati, series 7220 (Portale Antenati, ark:/12657/an_ua35925587/wQ31GEJ).
