Mazatlán, Sinaloa · 1898

Birth of Ramona Guzmán

Civil birth register, entry No. 427 — English translation of the handwritten Spanish record.

English translation

No. 427 — Birth of Ramona Guzmán. In Mazatlán, the register records a female child presented as the legitimate daughter of Manuel Guzmán and Tomasa Rodríguez, and given the name Ramona.

Her grandparents are named as follows: on the paternal side, the late Antonio Guzmán and Guadalupe León; on the maternal side, the late Juan Rodríguez and María López — the same four grandparents recorded in her parents' 1881 marriage act.

(The adjoining entry, No. 428, records the birth of an unrelated child, Ramón Esquivel, registered on the same page.)

Working translation of a handwritten civil register (three images of the same opening). The register entry dates to September 1898; Ramona's own later U.S. papers give her birth date as 7 September 1898. Source: Mazatlán, Sinaloa civil registration.
