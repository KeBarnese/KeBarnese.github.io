Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1909

Birth of Salvatora Carmela Barnisi

Civil birth act No. 51 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record. This child is Mary C. (Barnese) Karaba.

English translation

No. 51 — Barnisi Salvatora Carmela. In the year nineteen hundred nine, on the fifth day of September, at 11:15 a.m., in the town hall. Before me, Dr. Domenico Cimino, Mayor and Registrar of the Comune of Lascari, appeared Salvatore Barnisi, aged twenty-one, farmer, resident in Pachino, who declared to me that at 10:11 p.m. on the second day of the current month, in the house on Via del Prevosto no. 17, to Carmela Covelli — daughter of the late Francesco, aged nineteen, housewife, his lawful wife living with him — was born a female child, whom he presents to me and to whom he gives the name Salvatora Carmela.

Present as witnesses: Pietro Failla, aged fifty-five, farmer; and Emanuele Venturella, aged forty-one, teacher — both resident in this comune. Read and signed by the declarant Salvatore Barnisi and the witnesses.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. The act records the birth as 2 September 1909 in Lascari (registered three days later). The child, registered Salvatora Carmela Barnisi, is the "Salvatora Barnesi, age 2" who arrived with her mother Carmela at the Port of New York on 16 July 1912, bound for Saginaw, Michigan — and who in America became Mary C. (Barnese) Karaba. The record confirms her mother Carmela's age (19 → born ~1890) and that Carmela's father Francesco Covelli was already deceased by 1909.
