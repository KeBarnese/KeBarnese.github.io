Mazatlán, Sinaloa · 23 February 1881

Marriage of Manuel Guzmán & Tomasa Rodríguez

Civil marriage act No. 21 — English translation of the handwritten Spanish record.

English translation

No. 21 — Marriage of Manuel Guzmán and Tomasa Rodríguez. In Mazatlán, on the twenty-third day of February 1881, the term for the marriage publications (begun on 13 January) having elapsed and no impediment having arisen, the contracting parties and witnesses being present at nine in the evening:

The first, Manuel Guzmán, stated he is a native of Culiacán, twenty-six years old, single, a blacksmith by trade, currently employed as a public-order agent in this city where he has lived for six years, son of Antonio Guzmán (of the same origin, deceased) and of Doña Guadalupe León de Guzmán (residing in Culiacán).

The second, Tomasa Rodríguez, likewise confirmed she is a native and resident of this place, twenty-three years old, single, legitimate daughter of Juan Rodríguez (originally from Portugal, now deceased) and of María López de Rodríguez (of Copala, residing here).

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. Source: Mazatlán, Sinaloa civil registration.
