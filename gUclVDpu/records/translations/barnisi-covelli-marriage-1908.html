Lascari, Sicily · 25 July 1908

Marriage of Salvatore Barnisi & Carmela Covelli

Civil marriage act, Comune di Lascari — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

Marriage Acts. In the year nineteen hundred eight, on the 25th day of July, at 11:10 a.m., in the Town Hall of Lascari, open to the public. Before me, Dr. Domenico Cimino, Mayor and Registrar, there personally appeared:

1. Salvatore Barnisi, aged twenty, farmer, who has not yet fulfilled his military-service obligations, born in Pachino, resident in Lascari (Via Umberto no. 38 — the street is today Via Libertà), son of Giuseppe (resident in Pachino) and of Carmela Barnizri (resident in Pachino);

2. Carmela Covelli, aged seventeen/eighteen, farm-worker, born in Lascari, resident in Lascari (Via Umberto no. 35), daughter of the late Francesco [Covelli] and of the late Francesca Gugliuzza —

who asked me to unite them in marriage. Having read to the spouses articles 130, 131 and 132 of the Civil Code, and each having answered yes, I pronounced in the name of the law that they are united in marriage.

Present as witnesses: Salvatore Barreca, aged 55, shoemaker, and Emanuele Venturella, aged 52, shoemaker, both resident in this town. Consent to this marriage was given by the groom's father and by the guardianship council for the bride. Signed by the bride and witnesses — the groom having declared himself illiterate. (Registered 27 September 1908.)

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily records (Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese).
