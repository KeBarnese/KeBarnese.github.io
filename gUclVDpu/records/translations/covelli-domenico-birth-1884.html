Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1884

Birth of Domenico (Dominick) Covelli

Civil birth act No. 7 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record (a certified extract issued in 1908).

English translation

No. 7 — Domenico Covelli. In the year eighteen hundred eighty-four, on the twenty-fourth of January, at 3 p.m., in the town hall. Before me, Ignazio Abbate, Mayor and Registrar of the Comune of Lascari, appeared Francesco Covelli, aged thirty-five, trader (trafficante), resident in Lascari, who declared that at 1 p.m. on the twenty-third day of the current month, in the house on Via Torre, to Francesca Gugliuzza, his wife living with him — a farm worker (contadina), aged about twenty-six, daughter of Luigi [Gugliuzza] and of Rosalia Siracusa — was born a male child, whom he presents and to whom he gives the name Domenico.

Witnesses: [—] Giuseppe, farmer, and [—], trader, both resident in this comune. The declarant and witnesses did not sign, being illiterate.

Extract note: A true copy issued at Lascari on 10 January 1908 "for use of marriage," free of charge for the poor.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. This is the birth act of Dominick Covelli, Carmela's older brother: born 23 January 1884 in Lascari (registered the next day). It does three things at once. It confirms the parents — Francesco Covelli and Francesca Gugliuzza — turning Francesco from an inference into a documented father. It gives Francesco's age as 35 in 1884, placing his birth about 1848–1849, and his trade as a trafficante (trader/dealer). And it names Francesca's own parents: her father Luigi Gugliuzza (new to the tree) and her mother Rosalia Siracusa — the same Rosalia Siracusa who, widowed, would serve as the orphaned Carmela's guardian in 1908. The extract was pulled in January 1908 for Dominick's own marriage that year. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese.
