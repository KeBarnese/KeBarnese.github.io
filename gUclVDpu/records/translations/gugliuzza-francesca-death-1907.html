Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1907

Death of Francesca Gugliuzza, widow Covelli

Civil death act No. 1 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

No. 1 — Francesca Gugliuzza. In the year nineteen hundred seven, on the fourth of January, at 10 a.m., in the town hall of Lascari. Before Dr. Domenico Cimino, senior assessor acting as Registrar, appeared Luigi Maggio, aged twenty-two, farm worker, and Giuseppe Barreca, aged forty-two, mason, both resident in Lascari, who declared that at 5 p.m. yesterday, in the house at Piazza Garibaldi no. 28, Francesca Gugliuzza died, aged forty-eight, farm worker, born and resident in Lascari — daughter of the late Luigi, resident in Lascari, and of Rosalia Siracusa, farm worker of Lascari — widow of Francesco Covelli.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. This is Carmela's mother. Every identifier matches: Gugliuzza Francesca, widow of Francesco Covelli, daughter of the late Luigi Gugliuzza and Rosalia Siracusa — the same parents named in her son Dominick's 1884 birth act and the same Rosalia Siracusa who served as Carmela's guardian in 1908. She died on 3 January 1907 (registered the next day) at age forty-eight, placing her birth about 1858–59. Her death two years after her husband is why Carmela — orphaned at about sixteen — needed a family council to authorise her 1908 marriage. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese (FamilySearch image 3QS7-89MZ-8916).
