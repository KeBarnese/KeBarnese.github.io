Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1905

Certificate of Poverty — Domenico Covelli

Certificato di Povertà — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

Province of Palermo · District of Cefalù · Comune of Lascari — Certificate of Poverty.

The Mayor, acting on due information gathered, certifies that the named Domenico Covelli, son of the late Francesco, born in Lascari and resident in Lascari, is poor within the meaning of the law and belongs to a poor family.

In witness whereof the present certificate is issued at his own request, being poor. Lascari, 18 January 1905. The Mayor.

Working translation of a handwritten civil certificate. A certificato di povertà attested that a person was too poor to pay fees — it let them obtain official documents free of charge (Dominick's 1884 birth extract was later issued "free for the poor" for exactly this reason). Its genealogical value here is the phrase "figlio di Francesco defunto" — son of the late Francesco: it shows that Francesco Covelli had died before 18 January 1905, narrowing his death to the years between Carmela's birth in July 1890 (when he was still living) and this certificate. Source: Comune di Lascari, Palermo, Sicily (Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese).
