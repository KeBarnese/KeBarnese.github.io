Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1890

Birth of Carmela Covelli

Civil birth act No. 53 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record. This child is Carmela (Covelli) Barnese, Kevin's great-grandmother.

English translation

No. 53 — Carmela Covelli. In the year eighteen hundred ninety, in the month of July, before me, Ignazio Abbate, Mayor and Registrar of the Comune of Lascari, appeared Rosaria Miceli, midwife (levatrice), resident in Lascari, who declared to me that at 10:30 a.m. on the tenth day of the current month, in the house on Via Torre, to Francesca Gugliuzza, wife of Francesco Covelli, with whom she lives, was born a female child, whom she presents to me and to whom is given the name Carmela.

Present as witnesses: Salvatore [—] and another man, both resident in this comune. The declarant and witnesses did not sign, being unable to write.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. The act fixes Carmela's birth as 10 July 1890 in Lascari — one day earlier than the 11 July date the family had carried, and now on primary evidence. It confirms her parents directly in the Sicilian register: mother Francesca Gugliuzza, father Francesco Covelli. Because the mother is named as Francesco's wife (not his widow), Francesco was still living in July 1890 — the couple would both be dead by the time Carmela married in 1908. The birth was reported not by the father but by the town midwife, Rosaria Miceli, as was common. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese (FamilySearch image 3QS7-89MZ-5WR8, collection "Italy, Palermo, Termini Imerese, Civil Registration (Tribunale), 1862–1910").
