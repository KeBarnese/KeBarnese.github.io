Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1898

Death of Anna Covelli, age 4

Civil death act No. 21 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

No. 21 — Anna Covello (Covelli). In the year eighteen hundred ninety-eight, on the nineteenth of July, at 7-something a.m., in the town hall. Before me, Ignazio Abbate, Mayor and Registrar of the Comune of Lascari, appeared Giuseppa Marano, aged thirty, farm worker, and Clementina Gugliuzza, aged forty, farm worker, both resident in Lascari, who declared to me that at 2:14 p.m. yesterday, in the house on Via Teodoro, Anna Covello died, aged four, born in Lascari — daughter of Francesco, a day-labourer (bracciante) resident in Lascari, and of Francesca Gugliuzza, a farm worker resident in Lascari.

Witnesses: Giuseppe Cinquegrana, aged forty, farmer, and Pasquale Cesare, aged forty-something, farmer, both resident in this comune. The declarants and witnesses did not sign, being unable to write.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. This is the death of a previously unknown child of Francesco Covelli and Francesca Gugliuzza — a little girl, Anna, who died on 18 July 1898 (registered the next day) at age four, so born about 1893–94. She was a younger sister of Dominick (1884) and Carmela (1890) who did not survive childhood. Two things make the record valuable beyond the child herself: both parents are named as living in July 1898 (Francesco as a day-labourer, Francesca as a farm worker, no "fu"), which pushes the earliest possible date of Francesco's death from 1890 up to 1898; and one of the two declarants, Clementina Gugliuzza (40), was very likely a relative on Francesca's side. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese (FamilySearch image 3QS7-89MZ-5HFB).
