Lascari, Palermo, Sicily · 1905

Marriage of Domenico Covelli & Pasqua Auppa

Civil marriage act No. 7 — English translation of the handwritten Italian record.

English translation

No. 7. In the year nineteen hundred five, on the twenty-fifth of February, at 7 p.m., in the town hall of Lascari, open to the public. Before me, Francesco Napolitani, Mayor and Registrar, personally appeared:

1. Domenico Covelli, aged twenty, farm worker, born and resident in Lascari, son of the late Francesco — resident in Lascari when alive — and of Francesca Gugliuzza, resident in Lascari;

2. Pasqua Auppa, aged twenty-three, farm worker, born and resident in Lascari, daughter of Rosario and of Angela Gugliuzza, both resident in Lascari;

who asked to be united in marriage. Having read the required articles of the Civil Code and received the affirmative consent of both, I declared them united in marriage. Because the groom's father was dead and he was still a minor, the groom's mother gave her consent. Witnesses: Sante Isabella, 49, and Francesco Catanese, 29, both of this comune.

Working translation of a handwritten civil record. This is the Sicilian marriage of Dominick Covelli and Pasqua "Esther" Auppa, on 25 February 1905 in Lascari — several years before either emigrated. It corrects the couple's marriage date (previously assumed to be around 1908). It also fixes both ends of a family timeline: the groom is "son of the late Francesco," confirming Francesco Covelli was already dead; while his mother Francesca Gugliuzza is recorded as living and personally gave the marriage consent — so Francesca outlived Francesco and was still alive in February 1905. The bride's age gives her birth about 1881–82, and names her parents, Rosario Auppa and Angela Gugliuzza. Source: Lascari, Palermo, Sicily civil registration; Archivio di Stato di Palermo, Tribunale di Termini Imerese (FamilySearch image 3QS7-L9MZ-5Z7P).
