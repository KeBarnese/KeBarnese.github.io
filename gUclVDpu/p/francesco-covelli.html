Their story

Dominick and Esther's elder son, born in Sicily around 1908 and carried to America at five in 1913. His name is a quiet key to the family: by Sicilian naming custom the first son is named for the paternal grandfather, so 'Francesco' points to the Covelli grandfather — the same man who was Carmela's father, a Francesco Covelli.
