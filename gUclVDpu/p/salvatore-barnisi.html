Their story

The Barnisi patriarch who left Lascari for America. He sailed alone, arriving 11 August 1910, when his daughter Mary was not yet a year old — he had been there for her birth the previous September, but would miss her toddler years. Carmela and the little girl did not cross to join him until July 1912, almost two years later, by which time she was nearly three. He left an infant and was reunited with a child. The family name softened from Barnisi to Barnese on this side of the ocean.
