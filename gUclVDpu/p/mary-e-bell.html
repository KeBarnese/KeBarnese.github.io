Bell (Kentucky)

Mary E. (Hill) Bell

1849 – 1921
2× great-grandmother
Born
5 May 1849 · Morganfield, Union County, Kentucky map her death certificate gives her age as 72 years, 0 months and 11 days on 16 May 1921, which computes exactly to 5 May 1849. The 1900 census says May 1850 — a year out
Died
16 May 1921, 3 a.m. · 1419 E. Oregon St., Evansville, Indiana, aged 72 map cerebral haemorrhage of three hours; certified by Joseph H. Wallis, M.D. Her body was sent back to Morganfield for burial on 18 May. Indiana certificate no. 15742
Married
to James W. Bell — 18 Dec 1867 · Barrett Hills, Union County, Kentucky she married as Miss Mary E. Hill — her maiden name, recovered from the licence and the minister's return. Licence issued 16 Dec 1867; solemnised by Peterson Wall before Strather P. Hardy and Isaac N. McCormick; recorded Book 6, page 158. She was his second wife

Their story

As a young woman she was the second wife who stepped into a widower's home. Decades later she watched her own son Henry lose his wife Barbara at 28 — and a young Valeria step into that empty place, just as she once had. She lived the same story twice.
