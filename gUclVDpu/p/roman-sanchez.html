Roman L. Sanchez

1892 – ?
Great-grandfather
Older generations — On his parents: Roman's 1919 Los Angeles marriage license names Juan Sanchez and Agueda Lovato (spelled Lovato or Loveto). His 1916 ship manifest instead named a Domingo Sanchez as the relative back home — most likely a surviving uncle or brother rather than the father, since Roman was said to have been orphaned young. Naming their 1925 daughter Agueda independently supports the mother's name.

Their story

A fisherman's son from a tiny village in the Asturian mountains, he came to America in 1916 with his brothers and worked as a sailor, fisherman, and house painter. One brother is said to have gone to Argentina. Family lore held that he and Ramona were both orphans.
