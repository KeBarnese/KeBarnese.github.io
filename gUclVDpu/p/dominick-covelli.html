Their story

Carmela's older brother, born in Lascari on 23 January 1884. His WWI draft card names Lascari as his birthplace — the record that first tied the Covelli to a Lascari home — and his 1884 birth act now confirms it outright. He married Pasqua Auppa — known in America as Esther — in Lascari on 25 February 1905, when he was twenty and his father already dead, so his mother Francesca gave the consent. He then settled in Saginaw, part of the same Sicilian cluster the Barnese joined. Their little son Angelo died in Saginaw in infancy; their elder son Francesco was carried over from Italy at five.
