Their story

Carmela's older brother, born in Lascari in 1884. His WWI draft card names Lascari as his birthplace — the record that confirmed the Covelli were a Lascari family, and Carmela with them. He settled in Saginaw, part of the same Sicilian cluster the Barnese joined, and married Pasqua Auppa, who went by Esther. Their little son Angelo died in Saginaw in infancy; their elder son Francesco was carried over from Italy at five.
