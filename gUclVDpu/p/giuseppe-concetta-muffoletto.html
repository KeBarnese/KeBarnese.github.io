Their story

Salvatore's parents, documented at last. Sam's 1887 birth act in Campofelice names his father, Giuseppe Muffoletto, a shoemaker, and his mother, Concetta Di Paola — and the father's trade explains a great deal, since both Sam and his brother Tony carried the shoemaker's craft to Gary. Giuseppe was about thirty-five when Salvatore was born, which puts his own birth around 1852.
