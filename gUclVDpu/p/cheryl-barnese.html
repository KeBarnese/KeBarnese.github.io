Their story

Kevin's mother, born in 1957. She knew her grandmother Ramona Sanchez as a child. Her line reaches back through the Bells of Kentucky and the Sanchez and Guzman families of Spain and Mexico. By 1984 she was living in Canyon Country and made the local paper for a genuinely nice reason: she'd passed the Dental Assisting National Board's certification exam — some 300 questions covering hygiene, sanitation, radiation safety, and chairside and surgical assistance — fresh out of the dental assisting program at North Valley Occupational Center.
