Barnese · Barnesi (Sicily)

Francesco Covelli & Francesca Gugliuzza

1848 – 1905
2× great-grandparents (a couple)
Older generations — Documented now on both sides. Dominick's 1884 birth act gives Francesco's age as 35 (so born about 1848–49), his trade as trader, and names Francesca's own parents — Luigi Gugliuzza and Rosalia Siracusa — settling Francesca's birth around 1857. Carmela's 1890 birth act shows Francesca still the living wife of Francesco in 1890, and Dominick's 1905 poverty certificate calls Francesco 'defunto' — so Francesco died between 1890 and January 1905. Both parents were gone by 1908, when a family council authorized the orphaned Carmela's marriage with grandmother Rosalia Siracusa as guardian. One earlier guess needs correcting: because Francesca's father was Luigi, the Giuseppe Gugliuzza on that 1908 council (son of the late Rosario) was not her brother but at most a cousin.

Their story

Carmela and Dominick Covelli's parents, of Lascari. Francesco Covelli, a trader born about 1848, and Francesca Gugliuzza, born about 1857, both died before 1908, leaving Carmela an orphan — which is why a family council had to authorize her marriage, with her grandmother Rosalia Siracusa as guardian. The maternal line now runs Carmela ← Francesca Gugliuzza ← Luigi Gugliuzza & Rosalia Siracusa ← Giovacchino Siracusa.
