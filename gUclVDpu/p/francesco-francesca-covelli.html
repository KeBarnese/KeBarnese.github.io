Their story

Carmela Covelli's parents, of Lascari. Francesco Covelli and Francesca Gugliuzza both died before 1908, leaving Carmela an orphan — which is why a family council had to authorize her marriage, with her grandmother Rosalia Siracusa as guardian. The maternal line runs Carmela ← Francesca Gugliuzza ← Rosalia Siracusa ← Giovacchino Siracusa.
