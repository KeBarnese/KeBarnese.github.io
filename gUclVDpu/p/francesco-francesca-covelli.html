Barnese · Barnesi (Sicily)

Francesco Covelli & Francesca Gugliuzza

1848 – 1898
2× great-grandparents (a couple)
Older generations — Documented now on both sides. Dominick's 1884 birth act gives Francesco's age as 35 (so born about 1848–49), his trade as trader, and names Francesca's own parents — Luigi Gugliuzza and Rosalia Siracusa — settling Francesca's birth around 1858. The death of their little daughter Anna, age four, in July 1898 names both of them as still living — Francesco a day-labourer, Francesca a farm worker — so both were alive in 1898; and Dominick's 1905 poverty certificate then calls Francesco 'defunto,' which pins Francesco's death to the narrow span between 1898 and January 1905. Francesca outlived him by two years: her own Lascari death act records that she died on 3 January 1907, aged forty-eight, widow of Francesco Covelli — and it names her parents once more, the late Luigi Gugliuzza and Rosalia Siracusa, confirming the whole maternal line in a single record. Her death in early 1907 left Carmela, then about sixteen, an orphan, which is why a family council with grandmother Rosalia Siracusa as guardian had to authorize the girl's 1908 marriage. One earlier guess needs correcting: because Francesca's father was Luigi, the Giuseppe Gugliuzza on that 1908 council (son of the late Rosario) was not her brother but at most a cousin. Francesco's own death record, though, has not been found. The Lascari death registers were searched page by page for 1895–1904 with no Covelli, and the marriage allegati that would have documented his death (it had to be proved for Dominick's 1905 wedding) are not among the digitized Lascari records — only the 1862–64 allegati survive online. The likeliest reading is that Francesco died away from Lascari, quite possibly abroad, given the family's active Lascari–Chicago–New York–Louisiana emigration in these years. Confirming it would now take the 1905 marriage allegati from the state archive in Palermo, or a U.S. death record.

Their story

Carmela and Dominick Covelli's parents, of Lascari. Francesco Covelli, a trader born about 1848, died between 1898 and early 1905; Francesca Gugliuzza, born about 1858, outlived him and died on 3 January 1907, leaving Carmela an orphan — which is why a family council had to authorize the girl's 1908 marriage, with her grandmother Rosalia Siracusa as guardian. They lost at least one child young: a daughter, Anna, died at four in July 1898. The maternal line now runs Carmela ← Francesca Gugliuzza ← Luigi Gugliuzza & Rosalia Siracusa ← Giovacchino Siracusa.
