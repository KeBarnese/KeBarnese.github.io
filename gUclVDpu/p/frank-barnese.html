Their story

Enlisted in November 1940 and was at Hickam Field when Pearl Harbor was bombed, then served through the Guadalcanal campaign — a Saginaw boy who saw the war's first and worst days. He came home with a malarial infection that flared for years, treated with quinine. On an island where more men fell to malaria than to bullets, it was the part of the war his body wouldn't let him leave. He never spoke of the rest.
