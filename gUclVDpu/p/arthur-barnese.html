Their story

Arthur was the ninth child and, in the end, the last survivor of the ten. At the 1950 census he was twenty, living at 620 North Fifth in Saginaw and clerking in his father's grocery beside his brother-in-law Anthony Karaba.

Nine months later he married in California. On Saturday 27 January 1951, at Our Lady of Grace in Tarzana, he married Elena Ann Scala, daughter of Michele Scala of 18619 Collins Street — a hundred yards from where the wedding notice places Arthur's own parents, at 18512 Collins. His brother Anthony was best man; Frank's wife Tina was a bridesmaid. Both bride and groom had gone to Canoga Park High School.

The Valley Times account ends with a line that turns a society column into a war record: “Following a reception for 200 guests at the V.F.W. Club in Tarzana, the newlyweds left for Saginaw, Mich., where the bridegroom will be inducted into the armed forces.” He married and shipped out in the same week, in the second winter of Korea.

They stayed married fifty-eight years, in Chatsworth and Northridge. Elena died of cancer on 10 January 2009, aged seventy-seven; her obituary calls Arthur her “husband, best friend and loving companion.” He outlived her, and outlived every one of his brothers and sisters. When and where he died is not yet known.
