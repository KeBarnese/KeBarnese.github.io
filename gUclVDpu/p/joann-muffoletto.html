Their story

The last of Sam and Rose's five children, born at home on West 5th Street in Gary at five in the morning on the first of December 1933. She came early — the doctor ticked the "premature" box — and she was born into a family that had already buried two sons. The certificate says so plainly, in the cold arithmetic those forms required: five children born to this mother, three now living. Tina was twelve, Ella was four, and JoAnn made three.

Very little of her adult life is documented here yet. She married John Napoli, and at some point the family left the Calumet steel belt for Nevada — a long way from the shoe-repair shops of West 5th Avenue where her father and her brother-in-law both worked. She died in April 2019, aged eighty-five, and was buried at Davis Memorial Park in Las Vegas beside Peter Anthony Napoli, who had died two years earlier at fifty-three and was almost certainly her son. His marker calls him a beloved son, brother and father, which means there are cousins in that line still unaccounted for.

Her sister Ella outlived her by four years.
