Their story

Tomasa's parents — and a surprise in the tree. Juan Rodriguez was born João Rodrigues in Portugal, and crossed to the Mexican Pacific coast, where he married Maria Lopez of the mining town of Cosala. Maria carries the family's Indigenous maternal line: the mitochondrial haplogroup B2, a founding Native American lineage, traces straight back through her. Juan had died by 1881.
