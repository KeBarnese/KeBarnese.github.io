Their story

Frank's older sister, born in Lascari, Sicily on 2 September 1909 and carried to America as a small child on the boat with her mother. Her Sicilian birth act (No. 51, Lascari) registers her as Salvatora Carmela Barnisi — the same 'Salvatora Barnesi, age 2' who arrived with Carmela at New York on 16 July 1912. On Thanksgiving Day, 30 November 1933, she married Anthony M. Karaba, a Saginaw man, at Mt. Carmel Church before a large crowd — the bride in white satin with a veil fitted close in cap effect, carrying white roses and baby's breath; her brother Joseph stood up as best man. After a wedding trip to visit relatives in Gary, Indiana, and Chicago, the couple made their home at 453 North Second Avenue, Saginaw. That branch is the family's Karaba line, confirmed. She lived to 93.
