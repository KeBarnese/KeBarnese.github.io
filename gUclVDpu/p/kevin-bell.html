Their story

Kevin's uncle on his mother's side — and the uncle he was named for. Born in Los Angeles in December 1959, the second of Jack and Mary Grace's children, he had made his way to Ketchum, Idaho by the fall of 1979 — a ski town in the mountains, a long way from where he started. He was 19, not yet 20, when he was killed on 25 October 1979: riding south on Highway 75 just outside town, he slowed to make a left turn and rode into the side of a garbage truck. The driver, who wasn't hurt, told the sheriff he'd swerved but couldn't avoid the collision, and that Kevin's motorcycle had no brake lights or turn signals working. He was taken to the local hospital and pronounced dead that afternoon. He died young, but his nephew carries his name in full: not merely a Kevin and a William, but Kevin William — Kevin William Bell, and Kevin William Barnese, one generation on. He is not lost from this family. His whole name is spoken every day.
