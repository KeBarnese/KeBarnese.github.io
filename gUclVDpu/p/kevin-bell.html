Their story

Kevin's uncle on his mother's side — and the uncle he was named for. Born in Los Angeles in December 1959, the second of Jack and Mary Grace's children, he died young, in 1979. His nephew carries his name in full: not merely a Kevin and a William, but Kevin William — Kevin William Bell, and Kevin William Barnese, one generation on. He is not lost from this family. His whole name is spoken every day.
