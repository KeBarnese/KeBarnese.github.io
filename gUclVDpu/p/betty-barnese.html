Their story

Betty was the eighth of the ten and the second of the three daughters. In September 1945, at eighteen, she left Saginaw for Aquinas College in Grand Rapids — the only one of the ten recorded going to college. In April 1946 she stood as witness at her sister Frances's wedding.

By April 1950 she was in Los Angeles, twenty-three, working as a clerk for an advertising firm and living in Joseph's household with three of her brothers. Two years later she was back in Saginaw to marry Vincent Cozzolino — on 23 February 1952, the day after she turned twenty-five.

She spent most of her life in Southern California and raised three children, James, Catherine and Louis. Her obituary describes her as “a second mother to many,” a large-family cook who loved arts and crafts and holidays with everyone around. She died at Santa Clarita on 4 June 2011, aged eighty-four.

Her obituary does one more thing: it names her surviving brother as Arthur, and only Arthur. Anthony had died six months earlier and Samuel Jr. in 2007, so by the summer of 2011 the ten had come down to one.
