Their story

Born in Campofelice di Roccella, a coastal town a few kilometers from the Barnesi's Lascari. Two Sicilian families a short walk apart, split by an ocean, reunited a generation later in the American Midwest. He came over in 1911 with his younger brother Antonio and settled in Gary; after registering for the draft in 1917 (as a self-employed truck driver) he went on to serve in the U.S. Army during World War I as a Musician 2nd class — a bandsman — in the Headquarters Company of the 40th Field Artillery, enlisting in August 1918 and mustering out in January 1919, just after the Armistice. He came home to marry Rose in 1920 and raise his family in the same west-Gary neighborhood the family would stay in for decades. This branch still needs more documents from Sicily.
