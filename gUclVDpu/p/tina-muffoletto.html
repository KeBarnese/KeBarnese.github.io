Their story

American-born daughter of Sicilian immigrants, raised in Gary, Indiana. Gary is where the two Sicilian families met: the Muffoletto of Campofelice and the Barnese/Covelli of Lascari — towns only a few kilometers apart back home — reunited a generation later in the Indiana steel city, when Tina married Frank. She and Frank later moved west to California in the 1950s and ran the Pico Rivera Bowl bowling alley.
