Their story

Salvatore's parents. His 1908 civil marriage act in Lascari names them both — Giuseppe Barnisi and Carmela Barnizzi — and records the family as living in Pachino, in the far southeast of Sicily (province of Syracuse), some 250 km from the Palermo-area towns of the Covelli and Muffoletto lines. That Pachino origin is a real puzzle still to be squared with the family's Lascari associations, and it awaits a Pachino parish record to confirm.
