Their story

Salvatore's parents. Their son's 1888 Pachino birth act names them both — Giuseppe Barnisi and Carmela (maiden surname written Barnirzi there, Barnizri on the 1908 marriage act — the same name with the r and z transposed) — and gives Giuseppe's age as fifty, placing his birth around 1838. He gave his trade as industriante, and could not sign his name: the act notes he was illiterate. The family lived in Pachino, in the far southeast of Sicily (province of Syracuse), some 250 km from the Palermo-area towns of the Covelli and Muffoletto lines — a distance that makes how their son ended up marrying into a Lascari family the standing puzzle of this branch.
