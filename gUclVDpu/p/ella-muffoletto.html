Their story

Tina's younger sister, born in Gary in 1929 to Sam and Rose Muffoletto. She graduated from Horace Mann High School and was, by her family's account, a talented and accomplished opera singer. In November 1948, at nineteen, she married Phillip Pante Sr. — and she married into her father's trade. Sam Muffoletto was a shoemaker, entered on Ella's own birth certificate as such; Phillip ran a shoe repair shop at 611 West 5th Avenue and lived at the same address. The Muffolettos were at 543 West 5th by the time JoAnn was born in 1933, which puts the two families a short walk apart on the same street, and is very likely how the pair met. At the 1950 census the couple were still just the two of them, Phillip twenty-five and working, Ella twenty-one and keeping house. They made their home in the Miller neighborhood of Gary, where she raised her children and threw herself into community life — serving many years as a precinct committeewoman and, in 1969, co-founding the Miller Girls Softball organization. She was remembered as someone who never failed to have a smile on her face. She lived to 94, dying peacefully at home in Texas in 2023, surrounded by family, having outlived her husband and both her sisters, Tina Barnese and JoAnn Napoli.
