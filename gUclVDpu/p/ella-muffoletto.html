Their story

Tina's younger sister, born in Gary in 1929 to Sam and Rose Muffoletto. She graduated from Horace Mann High School and was, by her family's account, a talented and accomplished opera singer. In November 1948 she married Phillip Pante Sr., and the couple made their home in the Miller neighborhood of Gary, where she raised her children and threw herself into community life — serving many years as a precinct committeewoman and, in 1969, co-founding the Miller Girls Softball organization. She was remembered as someone who never failed to have a smile on her face. She lived to 94, dying peacefully at home in Texas in 2023, surrounded by family, having outlived her husband and both her sisters, Tina Barnese and JoAnn Napoli.
