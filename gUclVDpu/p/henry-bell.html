Their story

Henry's first wife, Barbara A. Bell, died in Evansville on 17 June 1917 at only 28 years old, leaving him a young widower with children. Six years later he married Valeria Hunter, a girl from back home in Union County — who stepped into the empty place much as Henry's own stepmother once had.
