Their story

Henry's first wife was Barbara — the 1910 census catches the two of them in Evansville, married four years, with an infant son, Thomas E., seven months old; a genealogical index for Thomas independently names his parents as Henry W. Bell and Barbara Bell; and Barbara's own death certificate names Henry as her husband, with Henry himself as the informant who reported her death. Henry was working as a bricklayer on houses in the spring of 1910. Barbara died young, on 21 June 1917 at their home at 910 N. Lafayette St., Evansville, 28 years and change old, of pulmonary tuberculosis; she was buried two days later back in Morganfield, Kentucky — the same small Union County town where her son Thomas had been born. Her headstone there reads "Barbara B. Bell, 1889–1916" — a different middle initial and both dates a year off from the certificate, a discrepancy left open rather than resolved in either direction. Six years later, in Sep 1923, Henry married Valeria Hunter, a girl from back home in that same Union County — who stepped into the place Barbara had left, much as Henry's own stepmother once had. She stepped into the same house, too: a birth announcement in the Evansville Press for late July 1924 records a son, James, born to "Henry and Valeria Bell, 910 N. Lafayette Ave." — the very address where Barbara had died seven years before. Henry hadn't moved on from that house; he'd simply gone on living in it, with a new wife and, within a year of remarrying, a new child. He worked the land earlier in life, later took a job at the Whirlpool Corporation in Evansville, and was a member of Grace Baptist Church. He and Valeria stayed at 1021 N. Fourth Avenue, Evansville, to the end — he died there at Deaconess Hospital on 3 June 1966, at 84, survived by Valeria, six children scattered from Los Angeles to Seattle to Flint, Michigan, 14 grandchildren, and five great-grandchildren. Among those six was Jack, by then of Los Angeles — Kevin's own grandfather, who had married Mary Grace Sanchez in Vanderburgh County twelve years before his father's death.
