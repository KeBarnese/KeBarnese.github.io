Their story

Kevin's uncle — the eldest son of Frank and Tina, born in 1946, about a year before Kevin's father, Frank Martin. His name is a small knot the whole family ties together: by Sicilian custom the first son is named for the paternal grandfather, and here both of Sal's grandfathers happened to be named Salvatore — Salvatore Barnesi of Lascari and Salvatore Muffoletto of Campofelice — so the one name honored both sides of the family at once. He was the third Salvatore in the line, carrying the great-grandfather's name down the generations. He died in 1991, at 45.

He made his living behind a drum kit. In November 1974 the Long Beach Independent put him in the Good Time Musical Revue at the John Wayne Theatre at Knott's Berry Farm — “Sal Barnese on drums and trumpet” — playing two shows a day, Monday through Friday. Two years later the Santa Maria Times found him up the coast at Rick's Rancho, keeping time for the Jerry Sun Show, a revue that had been the most popular thing in that town for five years running. He was a working musician on the California showroom circuit, in the last years when that was a living.

He married twice: Carol Fitzhugh in October 1966, when he was twenty and she seventeen, and Evelyn Diane Jones at Clark County, Nevada, in March 1977. He died at Los Angeles on 21 October 1991, aged forty-five — fourteen years after his father, and seven before his mother.
