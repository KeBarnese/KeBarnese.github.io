Their story

Roman's parents, in the Asturian mountains of northern Spain. His 1919 marriage license names them Juan Sanchez and Agueda Lovato. Juan seems to have died while Roman was young — the family remembered him as an orphan — which is likely why the 1916 manifest that carried Roman and his brothers to America named a different relative, Domingo Sanchez, as their contact back home.
