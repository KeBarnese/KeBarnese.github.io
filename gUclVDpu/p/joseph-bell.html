Their story

The last of the six, born at one in the morning at 314 East Tennessee Street — a three-room shotgun house on Evansville's east side, in the worst years of the Depression, to a father of fifty-four and a mother of thirty-four. His brother Kellie remembered being walked to an aunt's house before school that morning and coming home to find he had another brother.

His birth certificate does more work than any other document on this branch. It records him as fourth in order of birth, with four children born to his mother and all four then living — which fixes Valeria's family at exactly four sons and closes off any question of other children.

He registered for the draft as a sophomore at Reitz, from the North Fourth Avenue house where his father would die in 1966, but never served — alone among the six. He drove a truck, married Rita Faye at Owensboro in 1965, and stayed in Evansville the whole of his life. He died at home there in October 2012, aged seventy-six.

He and Kellie were the last two brothers living. He is recorded as Senior, so there is a Joseph A. Bell Jr. still to find.
