Their story

Dominick's wife — born Pasqua Auppa, known in America as Esther. She crossed the Atlantic in April 1913 with their five-year-old son Francesco, headed for Chicago. She is the sister-in-law first glimpsed as a name on Carmela's own 1912 manifest, before she had her own crossing a year later.
