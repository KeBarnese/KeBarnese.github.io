Carmela's sister-in-law (Dominick's wife)
Older generations — Her 1905 marriage act gives her age as 23 (so born about 1881–82) and names her parents: Rosario Auppa and Angela Gugliuzza, both of Lascari. Her mother's maiden name, Gugliuzza, is the same surname as Dominick's mother Francesca — a common Lascari name, so possibly a coincidence, but it hints the two families may already have been connected before the marriage.

Their story

Dominick's wife — born Pasqua Auppa in Lascari about 1881, known in America as Esther. She married Dominick in Lascari on 25 February 1905, then crossed the Atlantic in April 1913 with their five-year-old son Francesco, headed for Chicago. She is the sister-in-law first glimpsed as a name on Carmela's own 1912 manifest, before she had her own crossing a year later.
