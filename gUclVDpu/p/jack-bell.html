Their story

Known as Duke. He tried to get into the war young and was turned away — rejected for service on 19 February 1945, nine days shy of his seventeenth birthday — then registered for the draft the next year on 28 February 1946, his eighteenth birthday to the day, by which time the war was already over. He went to sea instead. By 1948 he was a sailor on the Great Lakes ore boats, crewing the S.S. Eugene J. Buffington for the Pittsburgh Steamship Company — U.S. Steel's iron-ore fleet — and registering for that year's draft up in Lake County, Minnesota, simply because that is where his ship happened to be. His card describes him at twenty: five foot nine, auburn hair, blue eyes. Ramona Sanchez's own son 'Babe' was a sailor too — the two families' seamen a generation apart. Kevin carries his middle name, William. He married Grace in 1954; like her mother Ramona before her, the marriage ended in divorce.
