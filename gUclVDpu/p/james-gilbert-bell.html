Their story

Valeria's first child, born on a July morning in 1924 at 910 North Lafayette Street — the house Henry had lived in with his first wife, and where she had died seven years before. He was known in the family as Gilbert.

He registered for the draft five months after Pearl Harbor turned eighteen, giving his address as his father's and his employer as the Bailey Bridge and Iron Company. In April 1943 he went into the Navy — the only one of the six brothers who did — and served until February 1946.

He spent his working life as a contract analyst for the Postal Service, married Clarice Jane Godby, and ended up at Clayton, west of Indianapolis. He died at home there in February 2006, aged eighty-one, after several years of dementia.
