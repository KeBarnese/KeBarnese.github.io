Their story

Dominick and Esther's younger son. He died in Saginaw in 1915, not yet three years old, of malnutrition — the hard truth of what the immigrant family's first years cost. He is recovered here from a single Saginaw death record; by naming custom, his name points to Esther's own father having been an Angelo. A short life, no longer lost.
