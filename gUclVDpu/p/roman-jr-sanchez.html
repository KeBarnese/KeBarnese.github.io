Their story

Known to the family as 'Babe' — the name is carved right into his headstone. Roman and Ramona's third son, born in 1926, he followed his brothers into the war as a sailor: a Seaman First Class in the U.S. Navy during World War II, and by the time of his mother's death in 1966 he was serving in the U.S. Merchant Marine. He lived a long life, dying in the San Francisco Bay Area in May 2015 at eighty-nine, survived by his wife Maria, his children Timothy, Roman, and Maria Ramona, and a grandson, Shane. He was buried with military honors at San Joaquin Valley National Cemetery in Santa Nella — the third of the three Sanchez brothers to come to rest in that same ground, beside John the Marine captain and James the Navy sailor. His marker carries the old sailor's line from Stevenson's 'Requiem' — 'Home is the sailor, home from the sea' — and, beneath it, the family's own farewell: 'RIP Babe.'
