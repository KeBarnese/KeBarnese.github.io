Their story

Crossed from Mazatlan as a girl of about thirteen, married a Spanish fisherman's son, raised five children through a divorce, and ended up running her own restaurant in Reno — where she lived her last twenty years. Her papers recorded her height at six feet: a formidable woman by any measure. Her obituary listed sons James, John, and Roman (in the Merchant Marine) and daughters Cecilia and Grace.
