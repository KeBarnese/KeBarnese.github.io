Their story

Sam's younger brother — 'Tony' to the family, and far easier to trace than Sam, because he left a full paper trail. Born in Campofelice in 1895, he came to America around 1911 with Sam and worked as a shoemaker; his 1917 draft card lists him employed by Sam and, with another brother, helping support their parents back home. He returned to Sicily to marry Anna — Marianna — at Campofelice in September 1920, and the couple re-entered the United States for good in 1922 aboard the SS America, their firstborn Giuseppe still an infant. They raised four children in Gary — Giuseppe, Paolo, Concetta, and Antonio Jr. — and Tony took out his U.S. citizenship in the mid-1930s. Widowed when Marianna died in 1956, he appears to have remarried an Emelia, who outlived him to 2011. He died in 1980 and lies beneath the Muffoletto family stone with both women, under the words 'Our father who art in heaven.'
