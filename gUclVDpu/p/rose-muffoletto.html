Their story

Long the hardest of the immigrant grandparents to place — 'just Sicily' — until a 1910 passenger manifest pinned her down, and she was not alone. On 23 May 1910 four people from Collesano arrived together on the Duca d'Aosta, bound for Chicago: her mother Giuseppa (36) and the three Macaluso children — Dominica (13), Agostino (11), and Rosa (8) — going to join her father, Alessandro Macaluso, who had crossed ahead to Chicago. Collesano is a Madonie hill town barely six miles from Campofelice, Sam's town: the classic Sicilian pattern of marrying the girl from the next village over, carried across an ocean and reunited in the Gary steel belt. The coherent family group all but settles her identity; her Collesano birth record would confirm it outright. She married Sam in 1920 and lived to 1991.
