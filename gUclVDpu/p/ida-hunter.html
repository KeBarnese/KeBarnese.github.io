Hunter (Kentucky)

Ida E. (Thomas) Hunter

1876 – 1956
2× great-grandmother
Born
11 Mar 1876 · Hardin County, Kentucky map exact date and county from her death certificate, and consistent with the 1900 census (Mar 1876). Her 1900 marriage bond instead gives her birthplace as Union County with her father born in Hardin — the clerk may have shifted the entries, or she gave the county she lived in. Hardin fits the family better: her father was a Hardin man and her mother a Meade woman, both central Kentucky counties, so the Thomases came west to Union at some point
Died
2 Dec 1956, 8:45 · at home, R.R. 5, rural Morganfield, Union County, aged 80 map of congestive heart failure over a week, from arteriosclerotic heart disease; attended by Dr. William Humphrey of Sturgis. Her son Austin Hunter was the informant, as he would be for his father ten years later. Some indexes give 1966 — that is wrong. She died in 1956 and William in January 1966, which is why his certificate records him as widowed. This page and the marker note previously carried 1966 for her
Married
married 13 Mar 1900 at J. M. Hunter's residence, Union County, Kentucky bond of one hundred dollars taken at Morganfield the same day, Henry as principal with G. H. Sutton as surety; rites by Alfred Cullen, Justice of the Peace, before Gus Greenwell and Jas. M. Hunter. Both are recorded as first marriages — which disposes of any suggestion that either had married before
Parents
John Thomas of Hardin County & a mother of Meade County ? her father is named on her 1956 death certificate; against her mother, Austin could only write “don't know.” The 1900 marriage bond gives the parents' birth counties as Hardin and Meade. He did not know his paternal grandmother's name either, ten years later — so the MacClure name from two of Valeria's sons' death certificates is still unplaced, and could belong to this Meade County woman
