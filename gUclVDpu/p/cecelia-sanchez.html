Her story

The fourth surviving child, born in a rented house on Harrison Avenue while her father was still calling himself an interior decorator and the family had two more years of owning a home ahead of them. She was eight when her parents' marriage ended.

After the divorce she and her younger sister Grace were placed with the nuns at Nazareth Home in San Diego. Grace remembered it bitterly and said the nuns were cruel. The 1940 census supports the account from the other direction: in April that year Ramona was renting on Auburn Drive in San Diego with only the two eldest boys, John and James. Cecelia, Grace and Roman Jr. are not in the household.

She married Carlos Jose Morales, a Nicaraguan, and had at least one son, Ralph Lester, born in 1950. She outlived him: he died at Walnut Creek at the end of 2006, and it was his mother who gave the information for his certificate, from the flat they shared on North Main Street. Her brother John had settled in Walnut Creek too.
