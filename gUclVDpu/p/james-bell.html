Their story

A man of Civil-War age with roots reaching toward Virginia. The story of Susan Bell belongs here: she appears to have been his first wife and the mother of his older children, before he married the young Mary E. who would later take in the widowed Henry and his boys. The exact shape of Susan's story is still being pieced together. His headstone in Morganfield — the same cemetery as Valeria's — gives his dates as 1828–1898 and marks him 'PVT.' A Confederate service record for a James W. / J. Bell in the 10th (Johnson's) Kentucky Cavalry, the 'Partisan Rangers' who recruited heavily from Union County, is very likely his — a Union County man of exactly his age. The full service file would confirm it beyond the common name.
