Their story

After her parents' divorce, young Grace and her sister Cecilia were placed with the nuns at Nazareth Home orphanage in San Diego. She hated it, and said the nuns were cruel. By 1950 she was in Los Angeles, living with cousins. She carries the family's Indigenous maternal line — her DNA showed mitochondrial haplogroup B2, tracing straight back through Ramona and Maria Lopez. She married Jack Bell in 1954 and built a family of her own. Like her brother John, she had red hair — a trait that most likely came down from their father Roman's Asturian roots in northern Spain, where red hair runs far more common than in the Mediterranean south. So she carried two inheritances at once: an Indigenous Mexican maternal line, and a head of northern-Spanish red hair.
